Tracking a DHL shipment

When we ship an order via DHL you will receive a tracking number in your confirmation email. To track your package:

  1. Open the shipping confirmation email.
  2. Click the tracking link (or copy the number).
  3. Visit the DHL portal and paste the number.

Tracking typically updates within 24 hours of shipment.
